PRIVACY POLICY

Last updated: November 2026

This policy explains how tyra667.github.io ("the Site"), operated by Tyra667, handles information when you visit and play DashR.

1. INFORMATION WE COLLECT

No account or login is needed. The only information you can give us is the optional nickname described below. Third-party services used by the Site may also process technical data, as explained in sections 4 to 6.

2. LEADERBOARD (SUPABASE)

If you finish a run and choose to submit it, your nickname (up to 16 characters), total time and death count are sent to a Supabase database and shown on the public leaderboard. Supabase processes your IP address for hosting and security. Please do not use personal data as a nickname. To remove an entry, contact us.

3. LOCAL STORAGE

The game stores your nickname and character colour in your browser's localStorage. This data stays on your device and you can clear it in your browser settings.

4. GOOGLE ADSENSE AND COOKIES

The Site uses Google AdSense. Google and its partners may use cookies and similar technologies to show ads based on your visits to this and other sites. Visitors in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland are asked for consent through a Google-certified consent message before personalised ads are served. You can opt out of personalised advertising in Google Ads Settings, through the DAA opt-out tool or Your Online Choices. More information: How Google uses data from partner sites.

The Site itself sets no first-party cookies.

5. GOOGLE FONTS

Pages load the "Press Start 2P" font from Google Fonts, so Google receives your IP address when the font is requested.

6. GITHUB PAGES

The Site is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may collect technical data such as IP addresses for security and performance. See GitHub's Privacy Statement.

7. CHILDREN

The Site is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their personal information. If you believe a child has submitted a nickname containing personal data, contact us and we will delete it.

8. YOUR RIGHTS (GDPR)

If you are in the EEA or UK you can ask for access to, correction of, or deletion of the leaderboard data linked to your nickname by emailing yakonixx44@gmail.com. For data held by Google, use your Google Account.

9. CHANGES

This policy may change. Updates are posted here with a new date.

10. CONTACT

Questions about this policy: contact page.
