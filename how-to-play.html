HOW TO PLAY DASHR

The goal of each level is to reach the exit as fast as you can. You respawn instantly when you die, so experiment freely.

CONTROLS

ActionKeyboard (default)
Move← →
JumpZ or Space
DashX (8 directions)
Wall-jumpJump while touching a wall
Restart levelR
Back to title menuEsc

You can remap move, jump, dash and restart in Settings on the title screen. On mobile, use the virtual joystick on the left and the jump, dash, restart, menu and fullscreen buttons on the right.

TIPS

  • Dash in the direction you hold: horizontal, vertical or diagonal.
  • Chain a wall-jump into a dash to cross wide gaps.
  • Deaths cost almost nothing. Use R to retry quickly and learn each layout.
  • After you beat a level, ghost mode lets you race your best run.
  • Finish the whole run to submit your total time to the leaderboard.
