WELCOME TO THE ARCADE

DashR Arcade is a small collection of free browser games written from scratch in vanilla JavaScript. No download, no account: click the cabinet and play.

DASHR

DashR is a precision platformer with 15 levels in three worlds: the Meadow, the Iron Caverns and the Desert. Run, jump, dash in eight directions and chain wall-jumps. You respawn instantly when you die, so the only thing between you and the exit is your timing.

Race your best run in ghost mode and submit your total time to the public leaderboard. It works on desktop with a keyboard and on mobile with touch controls.
